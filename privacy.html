《打个螺丝》隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月6日 · 生效日期：2026年11月6日

《打个螺丝》的运营主体为刘浩（下称“我们”）。本政策说明游戏相关信息的处理方式，以及您管理信息和联系我们的方式。联系邮箱：504064868@qq.com。

本政策描述当前独立游戏版本。该版本使用本地存档，尚未接入平台登录、广告、支付或统计 SDK。如后续版本新增相关功能，我们将根据实际处理情况更新本政策，并在需要时取得相应同意。

一、适用范围

本政策适用于我们提供的游戏功能和您主动联系我们的情形。第三方平台自身提供的账号、实名认证、运行环境等服务，由对应服务提供方按其隐私政策处理。游戏主动调用平台接口或接收个人信息时，我们会在更新后的政策中具体披露，不以平台政策代替游戏自身的披露。

二、我们处理的信息及用途

游戏在设备或浏览器的本地存储中保存各玩法的当前关卡及已完成关卡，用于下次打开时继续游戏。当前业务代码不将这些进度上传至我们运营的服务器，不在不同设备或运行环境之间自动同步。

当前版本未设置游戏账号注册功能，未接入广告、支付、排行榜或统计分析服务；业务代码不主动请求姓名、手机号、通讯录、精确位置、照片、相机或麦克风权限。颠钉子玩法使用屏幕或键盘方向操作，当前业务代码不调用设备运动传感器。

如您通过邮箱联系我们，我们会处理您提供的邮箱地址、问题描述及主动附带的必要材料，以回复和解决问题。请勿发送与问题无关的敏感信息。

三、存储期限与保护

本地进度保留至您清除相应存储，或运行环境在卸载、清理等情况下删除数据。清除后进度可能无法恢复。联系邮件在解决问题所需期间保留，处理完成后根据实际需要和适用要求删除或匿名化。我们采取与实际处理情况相适应的访问控制等保护措施。

四、第三方服务与共享

当前独立版本未接入第三方登录、广告、支付和统计 SDK。除处理您主动提出的请求、取得您的授权或适用法律要求的情形外，我们不会主动出售或公开您通过邮箱提供的信息。

本隐私网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问网页时，您的浏览器会连接 GitHub 的服务器；GitHub 可能为提供和保护服务处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息及访问日志，详见GitHub 隐私声明。本页面不嵌入广告、访问统计、第三方字体或跟踪脚本，也不使用 Cookie 或本地存储记录阅读行为；语言选择仅通过网址中的 language 参数读取。

五、您的选择和权利

您可以通过运行环境的存储管理功能清除游戏本地数据，停止游玩或卸载游戏。对于通过邮箱提交的信息，您可以联系我们请求查询、更正、删除，或提出个人信息处理相关问题和投诉。我们会在核实请求所需的必要信息后处理，不要求您提供与请求无关的材料。

六、未成年人保护

未成年人应在监护人指导下阅读本政策，合理安排游戏时间。不满十四周岁的用户如需通过邮箱提供个人信息，应先取得监护人同意。监护人可以联系我们，询问或请求处理未成年人提供的信息。如后续版本涉及儿童个人信息处理，我们将完善相应保护规则及同意流程。

七、政策变更与联系

游戏功能或信息处理方式发生变化时，我们将更新本政策；涉及需要您同意的信息处理时，将依适用要求告知并取得相应同意。如有疑问，请联系运营主体刘浩，邮箱：504064868@qq.com。

Privacy Policy for 打个螺丝 (Screw Puzzle)

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Effective: November 6, 2026

打个螺丝 is operated by Liu Hao (刘浩, “we”). This policy explains how information related to the game is handled, how you can manage it, and how to contact us. Email: 504064868@qq.com.

This policy describes the current standalone game version. It saves progress locally and has not integrated platform login, advertising, payment, or analytics SDKs. If later versions add these features, we will update this policy to reflect actual processing and obtain consent where required.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the game features we provide and to communications you initiate with us. Account, identity verification, and runtime services independently provided by a third-party platform are handled by that provider under its own privacy policy. If the game actively calls platform interfaces or receives personal information, we will specifically disclose that processing in an updated policy; the platform's policy does not replace our own disclosures.

2. Information We Handle and Why

The game saves the current level and completed levels for each mode in local device or browser storage so you can continue next time. The current game code does not upload this progress to servers operated by us or automatically synchronize it across devices or runtime environments.

The current version has no game account registration and has not integrated advertising, payments, leaderboards, or analytics. The game code does not actively request your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone permissions. The gravity-based screw mode uses on-screen or keyboard direction controls; the current game code does not access device motion sensors.

If you contact us by email, we handle your email address, issue description, and necessary materials you choose to provide so we can respond and resolve your issue. Please do not send sensitive information unrelated to your request.

3. Retention and Protection

Local progress remains until you clear the relevant storage or the runtime environment deletes it, for example during uninstalling or cleanup. Deleted progress may not be recoverable. Support emails are retained for the period needed to resolve your issue and are then deleted or anonymized according to actual needs and applicable requirements. We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to the information we handle.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The current standalone version has no third-party login, advertising, payment, or analytics SDKs. We do not actively sell or publicly disclose information you send by email except as needed to handle a request you initiate, with your authorization, or as required by applicable law.

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, your browser connects to GitHub servers. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information, and access logs to deliver and protect its services; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page embeds no advertising, analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. It uses no cookies or local storage to record reading activity; it reads the language choice only from the language URL parameter.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can clear local game data using your runtime environment's storage controls, stop playing, or uninstall the game. For information submitted by email, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise questions or complaints about personal information processing. We will handle your request after verifying only the information necessary for that request and will not ask for unrelated materials.

6. Protection of Minors

Minors should read this policy with a parent or guardian and manage their play time appropriately. Users under fourteen should obtain a guardian's consent before providing personal information by email. Guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning information provided by a minor. If future versions process children's personal information, we will establish appropriate protection rules and consent procedures.

7. Changes and Contact

We will update this policy when game features or processing practices change. Where consent is required, we will provide notice and obtain the appropriate consent under applicable requirements. For questions, contact the operator, Liu Hao (刘浩), at 504064868@qq.com.
